Should our VC fund invest in custom chips?

Amazon Web Services spent a decade learning to design its own data-center chips so it would depend less on Nvidia, Intel and AMD. Here is what you need to know before the debate.

Pro: invest Against: pass

From design to data center

AWS designs its chips but doesn't manufacture them. Tap each step to follow a chip on its way to the cloud.

  2. 1

    Amazon bought the Israeli startup Annapurna Labs in 2015 for a reported ~$350M. It now designs every AWS chip, working backwards from the workloads customers actually run.

  3. 2

    Partners help with parts of the design. In September 2026, Amazon signed a multi-generation deal with Qualcomm worth up to $60B in payments.

  4. 3

    Almost every advanced AI chip, Nvidia's included, is made by TSMC. Its 3nm lines and advanced packaging are close to sold out through 2026–2027, and Nvidia gets most of the packaging capacity.

  5. 4

    AI chips need high-bandwidth memory (HBM). It's in short supply and prices are rising, which squeezes everyone's costs.

  6. 5

    Chips are linked into huge systems. One Trn3 UltraServer connects up to 144 Trainium3 chips. AWS also installs racks inside customers' own buildings ("AI Factories").

  7. 6

    Customers rent the chips through the cloud. Anthropic and OpenAI have signed multi-gigawatt commitments to Trainium.

Amazon's chip family

Three chips, three jobs, three incumbents to replace.

Nitro

Replaces networking and security hardware

The quiet foundation. It handles networking, storage and security inside every AWS server, freeing the main processor for customer work.

3chip families built on its foundation
EveryAWS server runs it

Graviton

Replaces Intel and AMD server CPUs

A general-purpose processor for websites, databases and, increasingly, AI agents. Graviton5 packs up to 192 cores.

98%of top 1,000 EC2 customers use it
40%better price-performance (Amazon's claim)

Trainium

Replaces Nvidia GPUs

Built to train and run large AI models. Trainium3 (TSMC 3nm) is shipping and Trainium4 is in development. Anthropic's Claude runs on over a million Trainium2 chips.

144chips per Trn3 UltraServer
1M+Trainium2 chips running Claude
